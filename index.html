Fajr/Zhuhr/Asr/Isha for the 1st, 10th/DST-Sunday and 20th are taken directly from the mosque's published sheet. Fajr/Asr/Isha hold at that value until the next listed date (matching the board), Zhuhr is interpolated between them, and Maghrib is calculated from sunset (not on the printed sheet). Rows are highlighted when a time changes from the day before. Treat Maghrib and in-between days as close estimates rather than the official Iqama.
